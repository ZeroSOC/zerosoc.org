5 November 2026 Working Group

Third Working Group call: verdicts, gates and governance

The ZeroSOC Working Group met online on 5 November for its third call. Here are the main topics and where we need your input.

OCSF reserved UID 984 for ZeroSOC

OCSF has reserved UID 984 for the ZeroSOC extension. The extension adds fields only where OCSF has no concept of its own.

Near-miss or incident?

The group discussed one threshold question: did the malicious activity reach the environment? If it did, it is an Incident and needs a response. If it was stopped before, it is a Near-miss: the Case closes without remediation and stays on record.

A further proposal gives False Positives and Benign Positives severity_id 1 as well, keeping 2 to 5 for Incidents only.

Gates and the metrics they anchor

We reviewed seven proposed gates that take a Case from the alert to its closure, with T0, the first malicious event, before them. The gates anchor the speed metrics: MTTD runs from T0 to the alert, MTTI from the alert to the Incident declaration, or to the triage disposition when triage closes the Case as False Positive, Benign Positive or Duplicate.

Two questions are open. Should Mean Time to Contain start at T0 or at the alert? And should Mean Time to Respond stop at first containment, when the threat is eradicated, or when services are restored? We also discussed whether High and Critical Cases should allow bounded, reversible containment before the Incident is declared.

Governance and demo

The project is moving from a single maintainer to community governance, and we are looking for maintainers. We closed with a live demo of the reference platform handling a simulated phishing attack detected by Microsoft Defender.

How to take part

  • Not in the group yet? Email marco@zerosoc.org with a few lines about your experience and how you'd like to contribute to ZeroSOC.
  • Comment on the verdict structure in issue #3 by Tuesday 10 November.
  • Tell us where MTTC and MTTR should start and stop in issue #85.
  • Volunteer as a maintainer on the Working Group Slack.
